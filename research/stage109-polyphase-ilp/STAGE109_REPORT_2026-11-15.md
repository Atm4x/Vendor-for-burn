## What the supplied Stage 108 data actually shows

Both input ZIPs (`RVC_STAGE108_NSF_TRANSPORT_RESULTS.zip`, `exported_csv(3).zip`) were analyzed directly. Six CUDA benchmarks showed complete full-output parity. Benchmark median times (ms):

| Upsample | `single` | `kic` | `kic_ntc` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| stride 8 | 0.0715 | 0.0620 | 0.0855 |
| stride 10 | 0.1204 | 0.1236 | 0.1311 |

NCU median compute-kernel time (`gpu__time_duration.avg`, ns converted to µs):

| Upsample | `single` | `kic` | `kic_ntc` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| stride 8 | 49.26 | 49.81 | 59.82 |
| stride 10 | 117.90 | 113.63 | 123.60 |

KIC's stride-8 13.3% faster wrapper time is not matched by a faster compute kernel. `kic_ntc` is inferior in both shapes. Other NCU counters: FMA usage ~30%, L1TEX LSU ~63–66% (`single`, `kic`), occupancy ~87%, `Long Scoreboard` ~12.7–16.1 per issued instruction. NCU data includes **two kernel actions per report**, not a distribution over the entire NSF. Data do not show DRAM bandwidth saturation or actual FFMA opcode counts. NCU instrumented timings should not be compared directly with uninstrumented timings.

## Stage 109 hypothesis and algorithm

The remaining significant stall is from memory-dependent instructions. Merely permuting weight/input layout did not solve it. Introduce a new CubeCL kernel (`polyphase_pipeline4_kernel`) with four independent input/weight loads per input-channel group, a common stride-phase dispatch, and two compile-time accumulation modes:

1. `pipe4`: issue four independent reads in source, then accumulate to a single FP32 accumulator in the original order. This is intended to expose memory-level parallelism without changing numerical reduction order; compiler optimization may reorder loads.
2. `ilp4`: issue four independent reads and update four distinct FP32 accumulators, then combine. It deliberately changes floating-point reduction order and must satisfy full-output parity. The goal is higher instruction-level parallelism and fewer scoreboard stalls, at the cost of register pressure.

Both modes are available with original packed weights or KIC weight layout, yielding `pipe4`, `pipe4_kic`, `ilp4`, and `ilp4_kic`; existing `single`, `kic` remain controls. These are **opt-in in the isolated probe only**; existing production NSF remains unchanged. No claim is made that new modes are faster without GPU measurement.
