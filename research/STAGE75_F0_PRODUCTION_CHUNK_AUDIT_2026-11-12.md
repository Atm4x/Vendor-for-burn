Direct, Conv2d, Transpose2d and both produced 300 voiced frames out of 401. Local analysis of the full 401-element pitch sequences confirms no coarse pitch-bin differences for this independent cut.

**Unresolved:** Stage72 whole-WAV U-Net GEMM created PCM16 RMSE ~991 with large errors concentrated in portions near 40–50s and 80–84s, while the independent 80–84s F0 signal is numerically close. The two inputs are not equivalent: the full RVC first resamples and high-pass filters the entire WAV, extracts reflected/contextualized 300-frame chunks and batches them. The separate 4s test omitted that exact context. Tiny Hz differences can also propagate through the phase-integrating NSF oscillator, or a coarse pitch bin could change in a previously unmeasured chunk. A numerical Conv2d error is NOT established; production U-Net remains Direct until causality is proven. This also means the 8.535s Stage72 'both' route is not production approved.

## New Stage75 real RVC F0 audit

The optional `RVC_F0_AUDIT_DIR` hook lives in the actual `RvcV2Inferencer::infer_16khz_batch_with_f0` path immediately after CPU FAISS/protection/pitch-shift preparation, before TextEncoder and NSF. On actual chunk indices, it saves the exact final float F0 and 8-bit coarse pitch bins into per-chunk binary files with metadata. The default route is untouched when the environment variable is not set. GPU-resident F0 is explicitly rejected for this debug hook.

Run `Run-RVC-Exact-F0-Stage75.ps1 -Modes all` from the compiled Windows CI artifact after placing the five private assets under `.\embed\`. It runs original Direct U-Net, Conv2d GEMM only, Transpose2d GEMM only, and both GEMM, all over the same FULL audio and identical NSF All-GEMM + persistent GRU pipeline. It compares F0 per actual production chunk, including changed coarse pitch bins and voicing, and writes the full benchmark reports/WAVs.

```cmd
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Run-RVC-Exact-F0-Stage75.ps1 -Voice ".\embed\CrazyMita.pth" -Hubert ".\embed\hubert_base.pt" -Rmvpe ".\embed\rmvpe.pt" -Index ".\embed\CrazyMita.index" -InputWav ".\embed\MitaKind.wav" -Runs 2 -CoreFrames 300 -TensorBatchSize 2 -IndexRate 0.75 -Modes all
```

Output: `RVC_STAGE75_EXACT_F0_RESULTS.zip`, including `summary.json`, `<mode>.f0-chunks.csv`, all per-chunk FP32 F0/pitch binary files, WAVs, benchmark JSON and stderr. Different source/header files are NOT committed.

Do not interpret full-WAV times with an active F0 audit sink as equivalent to prior zero-overhead Stage72/74 performance. Only promote Conv2d GEMM after full-chunk signal parity, voice quality and end-to-end gain are verified on the RTX4060. CUDA Graph replay has still not been integrated into the real pipeline; continue once the reproducible baseline and F0 issue are resolved.
